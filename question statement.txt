All that you need to know about Snapdragon® AI Lab Build & Present Challenge
Participants are invited to submit proposals aligned with the AI use case development. The proposed solution must be designed, developed, or intended to be optimized for Snapdragon-powered HP PCs. A proposal may be newly created or, if it existed before the Submission Period, significantly modified to add AI models from Qualcomm AI Hub or other open-source platforms.

Visit Snapdragon AI Lab for more information:https://www.qualcomm.com/snapdragon/ai-lab


What to Submit
The proposed solution must be designed, developed, or intended to be optimised for Snapdragon-powered HP PCs.

Proposal Requirements

The proposal may be newly created.
If the proposal existed before the Challenge Submission Period, it must have been significantly modified to add AI models from Qualcomm AI Hub or other open-source platforms.
The proposal must be the work and/or idea solely owned by the participant.
Only one submission per participant is permitted. If duplicate or multiple submissions are received, only the first will be considered eligible.
A submission cannot be changed after it has been properly submitted on the Challenge Site. Participants should double-check all intake-form fields before submitting.
Evaluation Criteria

Criterion

Technical Implementation
Application Use Case &Innovation
Deployment & Accessibility
Presentation & Documentation
Tie-breaking: Ties will first be resolved by comparing scores in the first applicable criterion listed above, then the next applicable criterion as needed. If a tie remains across all criteria, the judges will vote.
